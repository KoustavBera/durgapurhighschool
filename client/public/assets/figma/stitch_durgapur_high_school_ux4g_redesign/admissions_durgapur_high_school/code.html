Eligibility Criteria

Age Requirement

Candidates must be between 5 to 16 years of age as of April 1st of the academic year.

Academic Record

Minimum 60% aggregate in the previous qualifying examination from a recognized board.

Residency

Priority given to residents of Durgapur and surrounding areas in West Bengal.

Special Categories

Reservations apply for SC/ST/OBC and PWD categories as per Govt. of WB norms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the medium of instruction? expand_more
The primary medium of instruction is Bengali, however, we offer English Medium sections for selected subjects in Higher Secondary classes as per WBCHSE guidelines.
Is there a hostel facility available? expand_more
Yes, Durgapur High School provides a safe and secure hostel facility for boys from classes VI to XII. Admission to the hostel is based on distance and merit.
Can I apply offline? expand_more
While online application is preferred, physical application forms are available at the school administrative office for those without internet access.
