history

Our Rich History

Founded in the post-independence era of 1954, Durgapur High School was established with a vision to provide quality education to the children of industrial workers and local residents. Over the decades, it has evolved from a small localized school into a premier higher secondary institution recognized by the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education. Our halls have seen generations of leaders, scientists, and citizens who have shaped the fabric of our nation.

Message from the Principal

Dr. S. K. Mukherjee

"Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think. At Durgapur High School, we strive to build not just scholars, but compassionate human beings ready to face the world's challenges."

With over 25 years in the field of education, Dr. Mukherjee has led various initiatives in digital literacy and vocational training across West Bengal. Under his leadership, the school has consistently achieved 100% pass results in Board examinations.
